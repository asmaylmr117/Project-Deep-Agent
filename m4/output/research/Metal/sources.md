For those of you who love power metal that has strong progressive tendencies, a lot of cinematic/symphonic layers, and a touch of some modern / melodic death-style heaviness in parts will be in for a real treat – as the material reaches that ideal upper level of excellence most ardent consumers prefer in this genre. For Chicago-area guitarist/vocalist Mark Sugar, his passion for 90s-oriented gothic, heavy rock, and thrash influences morphs into a darker, heavy/progressive metal template in the discography delivered to date since arriving on the scene in the mid-2010’s through Black Sites. The fifth studio album *For Eternity* represents a massive shift in terms of the lineup – José Salazar rejoins on bass, while second guitarist Lee Smith and drummer Chris Black round out the current quartet of players. Themes on the record run the gamut from social/political frustration at the hands of current versus previous generational outlooks, UFO cults from the past, all the way through to a celebration of this music we’ve all come to know and love. This is US metal with plenty of heart, well-equipped to capture a host of people seeking out something a little different that’s darker, melodic, hook-oriented yet progressive all the same.
